Presente y hábitos

Present simple / continuous + frecuencia

En Salamanca: I walk to school every day. Ahora mismo: I am walking to the gym. She always studies. Look! She is studying now.

¿Simple o continuous?

Mira la marca de tiempo y la idea: hábito o ahora.

Mini-check

Every day → simple. Now / today (en progreso) → continuous. He/She → -s en simple.

Present simple — hábito o hecho: I walk. She walks. · Present continuous — ahora: I am walking. She is studying. · always / never / sometimes — frecuencia con simple: She always studies. I never shout. · every day — todos los días → simple.
